## 1. The finding that shapes everything else

Median views by content type, computed from the 39 posts with legible view
counts across four profile screenshots (not full account analytics):

|     Content type     | Median views |
|----------------------|--------------|
| Footwork & culture   | 1,488        |
| Moorish education    | 316          |
| Civic & proof        | 228          |
| FundExecs & business | 107          |

Culture out-reaches business content by roughly **14×**.

This is a classification problem, not a discipline problem. Instagram's graph has
learned the audience shows up for culture, so capital posts are delivered to an
audience with no affinity for them, under-perform, and get throttled further.
A 62-view fund carousel is correctly-made content shown to the wrong graph.

Under a consolidated handle the fix is **bridge content** — culturally native in
format, carrying the capital argument as payload. It retrains the graph instead
of fighting it.

Follower count (1,958) is not the number to move. Qualified inbound is.

## 2. 506(b) constraints — priority one

> Not legal advice. Every item here is a flag for fund counsel, not a clearance.

### 2a. The website is likely the larger exposure

beygroupintl.com could not be loaded from the build environment (blocked by the
network egress proxy), so this rests on public search-engine snippets rather than
the live page. Those snippets describe an active $100M raise for BGI Fund I,
target returns of 18%+ IRR / 2.5–3.5× MOIC, and current deal/soft-circle status.

If that is on a public, unauthenticated, indexed page while a 506(b) offering is
live, it is very likely general solicitation — and a materially larger problem
than any social post, because it is indexed, dated and archived. **Verify what is
actually public, then gate offering specifics behind a login or NDA and leave the
thesis in front of it.**

### 2b. Legacy solicitation still on the grid

FundExecs Venture Club posts carry `refunder.com/fundexecs` contribution links, a
"Speak to a Rep" Calendly, and an Impact Fund I cohort announcement — publicly
soliciting money for a fund vehicle. Different vehicle from BGI Fund I, but prior
public solicitation raises integration questions about the offering that follows.

### Never publish while 506(b) is live

- That a fund is raising, open, or closing — in any wording
- Target fund size, IRR, MOIC, preferred return, or projected performance
- "Accredited", "LP spots", "allocation", "invest with us", "contribute"
- Links to a data room, deck, subscription docs, or contribution page
- A Calendly framed for investors rather than sellers
- Investor testimonials, or soft-circle / commitment progress
- Prior fund returns without full and fair presentation

### Fully open

- Market commentary on lower-middle-market M&A and succession
- Acquisition criteria and what you decline — deal marketing, not securities marketing
- The 100-day governance framework, in operational detail
- Operating history and how you learned what you know
- Soliciting **deals** from owners, brokers and bankers — unrestricted
- FundExecs OS as a software product, built in public
- Culture, civic and sovereignty work — all of it

### Remediation order

| Priority |                                                      Action                                                       |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Now      | Confirm what beygroupintl.com exposes publicly; gate or remove offering language per counsel                      |
| Now      | Archive (not delete — preserves the record) every post with a contribution or investor CTA; screenshot each first |
| Week 1   | Re-point every link in bio, highlights and pinned posts away from contribution pages and investor Calendly        |
| Week 1   | Rebuild portfolio proof without the raise — keep the six cohort logos, drop fund name, CTA and returns            |
| Ongoing  | One reviewer for anything naming a fund vehicle; everything else ships on the owner's authority                   |

## 4. Profile rebuild

### Bio

Current (131/150) — three titles, no proof, no differentiator, and a line
explaining that links are clickable:

```
Managing Director, Bey Group International • Founder, FundExecs OS •
Creator of The $100M Room.  Click the link below to learn more.
```

Proposed (139/150):

```
We buy profitable Midwest businesses and run them better.
Bey Group International · FundExecs OS
Chicago · operator, not a spreadsheet
Own a business? DM "DEAL"
```

Line 1 states the mechanism, line 2 keeps the brands, line 3 is the
differentiator both audiences care about, line 4 is a legal unrestricted CTA —
soliciting deals, never capital.

### Other profile fields

|    Field     |             Change             |                                                                                       Why                                                                                        |
|--------------|--------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Name field   | Keep `Sheik Astin Simmons Bey` | Separately searchable, and real name equity is worth more than keywords in a founder-brand strategy                                                                              |
| Category     | Public figure → Entrepreneur   | "Public figure" reads political; wrong frame for a cold seller                                                                                                                   |
| Following    | 46 → 250 over 30 days          | Biggest unforced error. Relationship business, following almost nobody. Owners, brokers, search funds, IB associates, family-office principals — engage two weeks before any ask |
| Link in bio  | Raw URL → two-door router      | Door 1 "I own or represent a business" → deal intake. Door 2 "Understand the thesis" → site. **No investor door.** Also gives click attribution                                  |
| Verification | Not verified                   | The dotted ring is the story-add ring, not a blue check. Meta Verified is cheap credibility for a fund principal                                                                 |

1. **What we buy** — acquisition box; highest-value highlight for deal flow

4. **FundExecs** — the OS as product, build-in-public

6. **Origins** — sovereignty and legal work

## 5. Visual system

Tokens pulled from `app/globals.css` and `tailwind.config.ts` so social matches
the product.

|    Token     |                  Value                   |                          Use on social                          |
|--------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Capital blue | `#1D4ED8`                                | Capital, deal and FundExecs posts — fills, CTAs, data           |
| Amber        | `#D97706`                                | Culture, origins and bridge posts. Dark ink on top, never white |
| Paper        | `#F0F5FC`                                | Default card ground. Blue-tinted, never pure white              |
| Ink          | `#091426`                                | Headlines and body                                              |
| Line         | `#B0C3DC`                                | Hairlines, rules, card edges                                    |
| Type         | Space Grotesk / DM Sans / JetBrains Mono | Display / body / numbers and labels                             |

**Template rules**

- One face per role, three faces total, no per-post exceptions
- Numbers in JetBrains Mono, tabular, so figures align across a carousel
- Blue for capital, amber for culture — never both as primary in one post
- Every carousel: hook slide, three argument slides, one CTA slide
- Sentence case for anything readable; uppercase mono only for eyebrows

**Retire**

- The yellow coin mascot — reads crypto-retail, undercuts a control-buyout thesis
- Bright yellow-on-orange cards, entirely
- The metaverse office renders — dated, and they contradict the AI-native story
- "Join now" / "Speak to a rep" button language
- Stock-photo event flyers with five competing typefaces

Venture Club, kept and re-cut institutional, becomes the community and education
tier beneath FundExecs OS the product — same blue, same three faces, same card
system. The mascot and every contribution CTA go with the old identity.

## 6. Content system

| Pillar  | Share |                               Job                                |            Audience             |
|---------|-------|------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Bridge  | 40%   | Culture-native format, capital payload. Retrains the graph       | Everyone — this is what travels |
| Capital | 30%   | Acquisition criteria, seller education, governance. Unrestricted | Owners, brokers, bankers        |
| Proof   | 20%   | Operating history, civic access, FundExecs build-in-public       | LPs and family offices          |
| Culture | 10%   | Run pure, no payload                                             | Chicago, community              |

|    Day    |       Slot        |                          Format                           |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Monday    | Capital           | Carousel — criteria, seller education, market read        |
| Tuesday   | Bridge            | Reel, talking head or VO over existing footage            |
| Wednesday | Proof             | Photo or carousel — operating story, room recap, product  |
| Thursday  | Bridge or capital | Reel, whichever the week's news supports                  |
| Friday    | Culture           | Reel, unmodified                                          |
| Daily     | Stories           | 3–5 frames: what you looked at, who you met, one question |

Five posts a week holds reach; it is not the growth engine. **The engine is DMs.**
Thirty minutes daily: engage the 250 accounts now followed, answer every deal
inquiry within the hour, never open with an ask.
